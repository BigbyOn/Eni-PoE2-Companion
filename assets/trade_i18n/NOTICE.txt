Exiled Exchange 2 translation data
==================================

The files `ru_items.json` and `ru_stats.json` in this directory are derived from
the Russian localization data shipped with Exiled Exchange 2:

  https://github.com/Kvan7/Exiled-Exchange-2
  renderer/public/data/ru/items.ndjson
  renderer/public/data/ru/stats.ndjson
